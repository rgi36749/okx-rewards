## What does “OKX rewards” usually include?

OKX rewards are not limited to a single signup payment. The platform currently groups several types of benefits under separate programs:

1. **New-user campaigns**

2. **Trade and Earn promotions**
   These campaigns can reward eligible users for trading selected assets, maintaining a net deposit, or completing a first-time trade.

3. **X Drops**
   X Drops campaigns distribute token rewards connected to selected crypto listings. Depending on the campaign, users may qualify by holding or trading eligible tokens during a stated period.

4. **Deposit Match promotions**
   These offers may provide a reward based on an eligible deposit, usually subject to caps, holding periods, product restrictions, or regional conditions.

The important distinction is that a campaign reward is usually temporary, while the fee schedule and VIP tiers are part of the platform’s ongoing account structure. A user may be eligible for both, but they are calculated separately.
